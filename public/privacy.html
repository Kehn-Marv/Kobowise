Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Kobowise, we understand that your financial data is highly sensitive. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your business information remains secure. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use the Kobowise Telegram Bot.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide our financial tracking services to you:

  • Telegram Profile Data: Your Telegram User ID and Username, which we use to identify your account and save your preferences.
  • Financial Inputs: The text, voice notes, and images you send to the bot for the purpose of logging transactions.
  • Transaction Records: Extracted data such as dates, amounts, categories, and descriptions of your sales and expenses.
  • Business Settings: Your business name, type, and bot preferences (e.g., language, notification settings).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for the functionality of Kobowise:

  • To generate your daily and weekly financial reports.
  • To categorize your income and expenses using AI processing.
  • To allow you to export your historical financial records.

3. AI Processing & Third Parties

Kobowise utilizes Google Generative AI (Gemini) to convert your raw messages, voice notes, and images into structured financial data. When you send a message, the content is securely processed by the AI strictly for extraction purposes. Your financial data is not used to train our AI models.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to any third parties. Your data is stored securely in our database and is only accessible by you via your specific Telegram account.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including database encryption and secure API connections, to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • Export your data: Use the /export command to download all your transactions.
  • Delete entries: Use the /cancel command to undo recent entries.
  • Delete your account: You can request full deletion of your account and all associated financial records at any time by contacting support.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by sending a message through the Kobowise Telegram bot.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us on Telegram via @KobowiseBot.
